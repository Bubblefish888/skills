# 15｜英文面试适配协议

## 适用场景

- 外企面试
- 海外岗位
- 英文自我介绍
- 英文 behavioral interview
- AI / 技术 / 商务英文面试

## 输出要求

每个英文答案必须包含：

- Natural English version
- Simple version
- Key vocabulary
- Follow-up questions
- Chinese explanation

## 英文自我介绍结构

```text
Hi, I’m [Name]. I have [X years] of experience in [field], mainly focusing on [core area]. In my previous role, I worked on [project], where I was responsible for [action] and achieved [result]. I’m interested in this role because [fit]. I’d be happy to share more about [strong project].
```

## 英文回答原则

- 不追求复杂词
- 优先清晰
- 句子不要太长
- 用具体项目支撑
- 准备 2-3 个过渡句

常用过渡：

- The main challenge was...
- My role was to...
- What I learned from this experience is...
- If I had to do it again, I would...
